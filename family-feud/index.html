    HOW TO PLAY

    1. The survey. We asked 100 people a question. The most popular answers are hidden on the board. Popular answers are worth more.
    2. Face-off. Two players race for control: buzz in (keys A and L) and type an answer. The higher answer wins and chooses to play or pass.
    3. Play the board. Whoever plays keeps typing answers. Each one found adds to the round pot. A wrong answer is a strike.
    4. Three strikes → steal. The other player gets one guess. Get it right and they take the whole pot. Miss and the pot stays put.
    5. Later rounds are worth more — double, then triple. Highest score wins and goes to Fast Money: five quick questions against the clock.

    Playing solo? No face-off, no steal — just you, the board and three strikes. Playing online? One player creates a game and shares the four-character code; everyone answers on their own screen.
